# ConceptLens

**Understand your answers, not just the solutions.**

ConceptLens is a student-focused learning website prototype developed by Team ThinkForge for the Seva First Innovation Challenge.

It is designed for CBSE students of Classes 9–12 to help them practise questions, review their mistakes, and understand the concepts behind their answers.

---

## Problem Statement

When students answer a question incorrectly, they often know that their answer is wrong but do not understand which concept they misunderstood.

Simply showing the correct answer may not solve the problem because students may continue making the same mistake.

## Our Solution

ConceptLens aims to help students identify possible gaps in their understanding through topic-based practice questions, explanations, and reflection prompts.

Instead of focusing only on right or wrong answers, the platform encourages students to think about how they selected an answer and what they need to revise.

## Key Features

- **Class Selection:** Supports Classes 9, 10, 11, and 12.
  
- **Subject Selection:** Students can choose from the subjects available in the syllabus data.
  
- **Topic-Based Practice:** Students can select a topic and practise related questions.
  
- **Multiple-Choice Questions:** Interactive MCQs with answer checking.
  
- **Concept Explanations:** Explanations help students review the idea behind an answer.

- **Mistake Awareness:** Feedback highlights possible mistakes and concepts to revisit where this information is available.
  
- **Reflection Prompts:** Students can reflect on how they chose their answers.
  
- **Practice Results:** Students receive feedback on their practice performance.


## How ConceptLens Works

1. **Select:** Choose your class, subject, and topic.
  
2. **Practise:** Answer the available practice questions.
   
3. **Review:** Check the answer and read the explanation.
   
4. **Reflect:** Think about the reasoning behind your choice.
   
5. **Improve:** Revise the concept and continue practising.
